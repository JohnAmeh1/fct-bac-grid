Our Mission

Digitising Property Tax for Bwari

The Bwari Area Council Digital Tenement and Business Rate System was created to eliminate the inefficiencies of manual tax collection. By moving the entire process online, we empower property owners to register, generate bills, and make payments from the comfort of their homes or offices — at any time of day.

Every naira collected through this platform goes directly toward community development, infrastructure, and public services that benefit all residents of Bwari Area Council.

About Bwari Area Council

Bwari Area Council is one of the six area councils in the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), Abuja. It administers a large geographic area covering communities including Bwari town, Ushafa, Dutse, Byazhin, Kwaita, Kuchigyoro, and many others.

Tenement and Business rates are the primary source of internally generated revenue for the council and are used to fund local infrastructure, roads, schools, and community services that directly improve the lives of Bwari residents.
