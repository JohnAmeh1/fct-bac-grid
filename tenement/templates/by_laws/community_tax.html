Community Tax/Rates, Development Levy & Allied Matters — Bye-Law No. 27

Part XXVIII — Bwari Area Council — 16th May 2022

It shall be an offence for persons, companies, corporate bodies, organizations, establishments or institutions (other than civil servants) that reside within the Area Council to fail to pay Annual Community Tax/Rates and Development Levy and obtain a receipt from the Area Council.

No. 27
Part XXVIII
16th May 2022
Magistrate/Area Court, Any Grade

Key Provisions

Collection, renewal, and enforcement
  • A receipt is obtained on application upon payment of the Rates/Levies prescribed in the First Schedule of this Bye-Law.
  • Payment is for the relevant year and expires on the 31st December of the same year.
  • Residents have until 31st March of the current year to renew payment; after which the maximum amount in the schedule shall apply.
  • Assessment of rates/levies shall be based on location or residence of the payer.
  • Where payment has not been made, the Area Council may on a Court order seal the premises of the defaulter (company, institution, etc.).
  • A defaulter shall pay twice the amount ordinarily payable; persons convicted may be liable to six months imprisonment.
  • No person after the First Quarter of any year shall be entitled to any benefit from the Area Council without proof of payment.

"Person(s)" includes a human being of 18 years and above, and any business entity with a registered office or branch within the jurisdiction of the Area Council.
