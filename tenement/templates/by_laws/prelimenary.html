Short Title and Commencement

Part I — General Provisions

In accordance with the provisions of Section 7 and Fourth Schedule to the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended) and the relevant provisions of the Area Council Act, Laws of Federal Capital Territory, Abuja 2006, and other applicable enabling Laws, the Bwari Area Council promulgates the Area Council's Bye-Laws in the interest and for the promotion of the welfare and unity of the people of Bwari Area Council.

This Bye-Law and any amendment(s) therein may be cited as Bwari Area Council Bye-Law 2016, and shall come into force 5th December, 2016.

Bwari Area Council Bye-Law 2016
5th December, 2016
Bwari Area Council, FCT Abuja
