PSPRO Bye-Law No. 21 of 2016

Part XXII — Bwari Area Council FCT Abuja, Nigeria

Pursuant to the provisions of Section 7 and the Fourth Schedule to the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999, and other relevant sections of the Local Government Act, Laws of FCT Abuja 2006, this Bye-Law regulates private sector participation in refuse collection, transportation and disposal within the Bwari Area Council.

No. 21 of 2016
Part XXII
5th January 2016
Section 7, Fourth Schedule — 1999 Constitution

Mandatory Engagement of PSP Refuse Operators

Section 1 — Obligation on Property Owners & Occupiers

Every owner or occupier of premises, buildings and tenements shall engage the services of a Private Sector Participation (PSP) Refuse Operator that is registered and/or licensed by the Bwari Area Council for the purpose of removal, transportation and disposal of refuse from such building, premises and/or operations within any part of the territorial jurisdiction of the Area Council.

Only PSP Refuse Operators registered and licensed by the Bwari Area Council are authorised to operate within the territorial jurisdiction of the Area Council.

Prohibited Acts & Offences

Sections 2–4 — Offences
  • Unregistered Operation: It shall be an offence for any unregistered PSP Refuse Operator to carry on business and/or operations within any part of the territorial jurisdiction of the Area Council.
  • Illegal Disposal: It shall be an offence for any owner or occupier of any building or tenement to dispose of refuse other than through a PSP operator registered by Bwari Area Council.
  • Obstruction: It shall be an offence for any person, whether an individual or a body corporate or unincorporated, to obstruct a registered and/or licensed PSP Refuse Operator or its agent or delegate in the process of performing their legitimate duty of refuse collection.
Contraventions of any section of this Bye-Law are punishable as offences. The court with jurisdiction is the Magistrate Court of any grade.
