Radio/Television & Communication Mast Bye-Law No. 15 of 2016

Part XVI — Bwari Area Council FCT Abuja, Nigeria

Any person, company or establishment that owns or controls a radio or television instrument and/or communication mast or other items of a similar kind that use radio waves within the territorial jurisdiction of Bwari Area Council shall pay an annual License fee to the Bwari Area Council.

No. 15 of 2016
Part XVI
5th January 2016
Section 7, Fourth Schedule — 1999 Constitution

Licensing Provisions

Sections 1–5 — Fee Basis & Inspection Powers
  • The annual license fee depends on whether the use is private or commercial, and on the volume or number of instruments/items on the premises.
  • The fee amounts shall be in accordance with the conditions contained in the First Schedule to this Bye-Law.
  • Authorized officers or agents of Bwari Area Council shall have the power to enter any premises — residential or commercial — during working hours for the purpose of inspection.
  • It shall be an offence to obstruct any Area Council official or authorized agent in the discharge of their duties under this Bye-Law.
Individuals found in contravention shall be liable to a fine of 50% of the amount due plus the fee owed, or three months imprisonment, or both. Corporate bodies shall face a fine of 50% of the amount due plus fees owed, or closure for up to one month, or both.
