Environmental Sanitation

Sanitation Levy &
Monthly Trash Pickup

The Bwari Area Council Environmental Sanitation Levy is a mandatory fee collected from all residential and commercial premises within the council area. It funds organised waste collection, neighbourhood cleanliness, and environmental health enforcement.

When you pay your sanitation levy through this portal, your property is automatically enrolled in the monthly doorstep refuse collection programme — a council sanitation team visits your registered address every month to collect household waste at no additional charge.

What is Tenement Rate?

Tenement rate is an annual property tax levied by the Bwari Area Council on all residential and commercial buildings within its jurisdiction, as required by Nigerian law. It is used to fund local infrastructure and services.

Who is required to pay?

All owners of residential houses, commercial buildings, and landed properties within Bwari Area Council are legally required to pay the annual tenement rate. Failure to pay may attract penalties.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payments are processed through Paystack — the Nigerian government's approved payment gateway. You can pay using your debit/credit card, internet banking, USSD, or direct bank deposit using your unique RRR reference.

How do I get my receipt?

After successful payment, your official receipt is immediately available for download from your dashboard. The receipt is digitally stamped and accepted as proof of compliance by the Bwari Area Council.

What happens if I don't pay before the deadline?

Properties with outstanding rates may be subject to penalties, surcharges, or enforcement actions by the Area Council. We recommend paying early to avoid additional fees.
