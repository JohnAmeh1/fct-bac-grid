Overview

What Is the Sanitation Levy?

The Bwari Area Council Environmental Sanitation Levy is a mandatory annual fee imposed on all residential and commercial properties within the Bwari Area Council jurisdiction. It is authorised under the Environmental Sanitation Bye-Law (Bye-Law No. 4 of 2016) and is administered by the Council's Department of Environmental Health.

Revenue from the levy funds organised refuse collection, environmental inspections, drainage maintenance, public toilet upkeep, and sanitation outreach across all Bwari districts including Bwari, Byazhin, Ushafa, Kauran Mashi, Rubochi, and surrounding communities.

Every property owner and registered landlord in the Area Council is legally required to pay the sanitation levy annually. Non-compliance attracts fines, prohibition notices, and enforcement visits from the Environmental Health Officers.

Legal Authority

The levy is backed by the Bwari Area Council Environmental Sanitation Bye-Law No. 4 of 2016, Part V — which governs premises inspection, prohibition of indiscriminate refuse dumping, and environmental health offences within the council area. Read the full bye-law →

Non-Payment Penalty Notice

Under the Environmental Sanitation Bye-Law, properties that fail to pay the sanitation levy are subject to enforcement action including fines of up to ₦50,000, prohibition notices, and court proceedings. FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is required to pay the Sanitation Levy?

All property owners, landlords, and business operators within the Bwari Area Council jurisdiction are legally required to pay the annual sanitation levy. This includes residential property owners, tenement landlords, commercial business owners, and market traders.

What payment methods are accepted?

The portal processes payments via Paystack, which supports debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfer, USSD (*737#, *822#), and mobile money. All transactions are encrypted and secure. You receive an instant payment confirmation and downloadable receipt.

When does the monthly trash pickup start after payment?

Your property is added to the monthly refuse collection route within 48 hours of successful payment confirmation. Your GPS-registered address is used to schedule the sanitation crew's route. You will receive your first pickup in the next scheduled monthly round for your district.

Can I pay the levy for multiple properties?

Yes. You can register multiple properties under a single portal account and pay the sanitation levy for each property individually. Each property will receive its own compliance receipt and will be enrolled in the monthly collection programme separately.

What happens if the sanitation crew misses my pickup?

If you experience a missed pickup, you may submit a complaint directly through your portal dashboard under the "Complaints" section. Your complaint will be routed to the Environmental Health Department for resolution, and a makeup collection will be scheduled.

What happens if I do not pay the sanitation levy?

Non-payment of the sanitation levy is an offence under the Bwari Environmental Sanitation Bye-Law. Defaulting properties are subject to fines, prohibition notices from Environmental Health Officers, and court summons. Properties without a valid levy receipt may also fail environmental compliance inspections required for business licence renewals and property transactions.

Is the sanitation levy the same as the tenement rate?

No. The tenement rate is a property-based annual tax levied on the occupation of premises. The sanitation levy is a separate environmental services fee that specifically funds refuse collection, drainage maintenance, and environmental health operations. Both must be paid independently. You can pay both through the same portal account.

How do I download my sanitation levy receipt?

After a successful payment, your receipt is available immediately on your payments dashboard. Navigate to "Payments" in the sidebar, locate your sanitation levy transaction, and click "Download Receipt" to get a PDF copy. You can also use the public receipt verification tool on the homepage by entering your payment reference number.
